Release notes

1.0.0

The first release of Holdout4Ever.

  • Ten levels in the Salvage Yard, with the mini-boss Scrapjaw halfway and the chapter boss The Grinder at the end.
  • Gates that boost your damage, fire rate or shots, heal you, or set off a bomb.
  • A base that keeps producing while you are away, and an arsenal of weapons and perks to upgrade.
  • Two play styles: Relaxed, where your hero fires on its own, and Active, where you tap to shoot.
  • A How to play guide in Settings.
  • Free to play with ads. Optional purchases remove the ads between runs.
