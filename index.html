Holdout4Ever

A one-thumb lane shooter for iPhone.

Join the beta on TestFlight

Free beta for iPhone. You'll need Apple's TestFlight app.

The game

Steer your hero left and right while they fire automatically. Grab upgrades along the lane, survive waves of scrap-built enemies, and between runs build and upgrade your base to come back stronger.

The beta contains Chapter 1, Salvage Yard: 10 levels and two boss fights.

How to join

  • Open this page on your iPhone and tap Join the beta.
  • Install TestFlight from the App Store if you don't have it.
  • Accept the invitation and tap Install.

Feedback: take a screenshot in the game, tap it, and choose Share Beta Feedback.
