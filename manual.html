How to play

Your goal

Your hero runs up the road on their own. You win a level by reaching the finish line or, on a boss level, by defeating the boss, before your health runs out. Each level card says which. On a boss level you also have a time limit: if the boss is still standing when time runs out, the run is lost.

Steering

Drag left or right anywhere on the screen to steer.

Play styles: Relaxed and Active

In Relaxed, your hero fires automatically. In Active, your hero fires only when you tap, about 3 taps a second for full fire. A quick tap never moves your hero, and with two fingers one can steer while the other taps. Choose your play style above the level list; you can switch between runs.

Gates

Rows of gates cross the road. You get the gate you drive through, and the rest of that row is gone.

  • Multishot gates make you fire more shots at once.
  • Damage and fire rate gates make your shots hit harder or come faster. A gate with a minus makes you weaker: steer around it.
  • These boosts last a short time, then wear off.
  • Heal gates give back health at once.
  • A bomb destroys up to eight of the hostiles nearest to you (more with Payload) and hurts any boss or big hostile in sight.

While a boss is alive, rows of helpful gates keep coming.

Weapons

There are four weapons. You start with the Carbine, which fires a steady stream of bullets. Level 1-1 always uses the Carbine.

The Scatter fires a single pellet until Multishot gates spread more pellets out, and each pellet can pass through one hostile. Win level 1-10 to unlock it, then buy it with Scrap in the Arsenal.

The Lance and the Mortar are locked in this version; their cards in the Arsenal show which chapter unlocks them.

Hostiles and bosses

Most hostiles walk toward you, and most that reach you cost you health, as does any that gets past you. Bigger hostiles stop ahead of you and attack. A warning shows where an attack will land a moment before it hits, so steer out of it; a few small hits are too wide to dodge.

Level 1-5 ends with the mini-boss Scrapjaw, who charges down your lane. Level 1-10 ends with the chapter boss The Grinder, whose armor stops weak hits. Both call in more hostiles once they have lost part of their health.

Health, defeat and revive

You start every run with full health. Hostiles and their attacks take health away; Heal gates give it back. When your health reaches zero, the run is lost, unless you revive.

When a revive is offered, you can take it once per run: watch a short ad (with Ad-Free Plus there is no ad, within the same daily limits) and you come back with full health, keep your gate boosts, and the hostiles are pushed back. If no ad can be shown, for example without a connection or after the daily limit, the button says why and you cannot revive that time. A win after a revive earns at most two stars.

Stars, levels and chapters

A win earns one to three stars: the more health you have left at the end, the more stars. Your best result on each level is kept.

Each level opens when you win the level before it; stars never lock a level. Stars add up for each chapter, and the milestones below the chapter's levels give Power and Scrap, and the last one a new look for the road. This version has one chapter, the Salvage Yard, with ten levels. More chapters are coming later.

Every win pays Scrap: more for more stars and for every hostile you defeat, plus a bonus the first time you win a level. A lost run pays less, for the hostiles you defeated. A run you abandon from the pause menu pays nothing.

The base

Your base makes the resources you spend. The Farm makes Food, the Generator makes Power and the Scrapyard makes Scrap. Each one keeps working while you are away, even with the game closed, until its own storage is full; select it and tap Collect. The Warehouse raises how much you can keep.

Upgraded buildings make more and hold more. Building and upgrading cost resources and take time. You have two builders, so two jobs can run at once. Upgrading the HQ unlocks new buildings, and no other building can go above the HQ's level.

The arsenal

In the Arsenal you unlock and upgrade weapons and buy perks with Scrap and Power. Each weapon level adds damage, and the Armory's level limits how high your weapons can go. Tap Use in runs to pick the weapon for your next runs; a run starts with it at its level.

Perks:

  • Plating gives you more health.
  • Payload makes each bomb destroy one more hostile per level.
  • Second Wind makes a revive push hostiles further back.

The Arsenal shows your power rating. If a level card says Underpowered, upgrade your weapon or perks first; you can still play the level.
