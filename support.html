Support

Report a problem or send feedback

In the beta, the fastest way is inside the game: take a screenshot, tap the preview and choose Share Beta Feedback. Your note, the screenshot and your device model reach us directly.

You can also email holdout (at) dadgar.se. We reply within 3 working days.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved?
On your iPhone only. It is never sent to us, and the game has no account.

Is my progress in my iPhone's backup? How do I move to a new iPhone?
Yes, your progress is part of your iPhone's normal backup (iCloud or a computer). To move to a new iPhone, set it up from a backup of your old one, or transfer directly from the old iPhone; your progress comes with it. There is no other way to move it, since there is no account.

What happens if I delete the app?
Your progress on that iPhone is deleted. A backup made before you deleted it still holds it. Purchases are not lost: see "How do I restore my purchases?".

What are Relaxed and Active?
Two play styles. In Relaxed, your hero fires automatically and you only steer. In Active, you tap to shoot. Switch between them above the level list, between runs.

Why is there no sound?
The game follows your iPhone's silent mode: when the iPhone is set to silent, the game is silent too, and music from other apps keeps playing. Set the music and effects volume in the game under Settings → Sound.

How do I restore my purchases?
In the game, open Settings → Remove ads and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. Refunds and payment questions are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Why do I see ads, and how do I change my privacy choices?
The game is free and shows ads from Google; "Remove Ads" and "Ad-Free Plus" in Settings → Remove ads change that. Where your region requires it, change your ad privacy choices in the game under Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices, and the tracking permission in the iPhone's Settings under Privacy & Security → Tracking. The privacy policy explains what each choice means.

Does the game need the internet?
No. You can play fully offline. Ads, and rewards you choose to watch an ad for, need a connection; so do purchases.

The beta link says it's full or unavailable.
The beta may be paused between builds or have reached its tester limit. Try again later.
