Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Holdout4Ever has no accounts and no analytics, and we (the developer) receive no personal data from the game. The game shows ads from Google, and this page explains what that means.

Ads

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Before the first ad, Google's consent tool asks for your choice where the law requires it, for example in the EU, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices.

Ads are never personalised. The game asks Google for non-personalised ads only. These ads are chosen from the game itself and your approximate location (for example your city), not from your past behaviour.

Tracking permission. After your privacy choice, the game asks once whether Holdout4Ever may allow tracking: first a short explanation, then Apple's own question. If you allow it, Google may use your device's advertising identifier and link it with data from other companies' apps and websites, for example to measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad and prevent fraud. The ads stay non-personalised either way, and the game is the same whatever you choose. You can change your answer at any time in the iPhone's Settings under Privacy & Security → Tracking. The game itself never reads the identifier and shares no data with data brokers.

The game never asks about tracking if you said no in the privacy choice or refused Google there, if you shared an age range under 18, or if you have Ad-Free Plus.

What Google receives when an ad is requested or shown: your IP address; an identifier that Google stores in this app (a cookie used to limit how often you see the same ad and to count the ads shown); your approximate location, worked out from your internet connection; which ads you saw and whether you tapped them; and technical data such as performance and crash information. Google handles this under its own privacy policy and its page on how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Age. The game never asks you to type your age. On supported devices it may ask, through Apple, whether you want to share an age range. If you share it, the game uses it only for ad settings: with each ad request it tells Google whether to treat the request as for a child or for a teen, with a matching stricter rating for ad content, and it never asks a child or a teen about tracking. The game keeps the age range only while it is running, never stores it, and never sends the age range itself, only that child or teen setting.

Removing ads. "Remove Ads" stops the ads between runs; ads you choose to watch for a reward stay optional. "Ad-Free Plus" gives those rewards without an ad. With Ad-Free Plus the game requests no ads, does not start Google's ad software and never asks about tracking. Google's consent tool still checks your privacy choice once each time the game starts, so you can still change it in Settings.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Your game progress

Your levels, stars and base are stored only on your device, together with the game's log of ads and purchases. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Nothing else is shared

The game has no analytics and no other third-party services. Purchases and the age-range question go through Apple. If your network blocks ad services, the game still works; the ad buttons are just unavailable.

Beta testing

If you test through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares limited information with us: for example your name or email if you joined by invitation, your device model, crash reports and any feedback you choose to send. This is handled under Apple's privacy policy, and we use it only to improve the game.

Children

The game has no accounts and asks no one for personal data. If a device shares an age range under 13, the game tells Google to treat ad requests as for a child, which limits the ads shown, and it never asks about tracking.
