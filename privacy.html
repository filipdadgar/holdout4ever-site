Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Holdout4Ever does not collect, store or share any personal data.

No accounts, no tracking, no ads, no analytics

The game does not connect to the internet.

Your game progress

Your levels, stars and base are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Beta testing

If you test through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares limited information with us: for example your name or email if you joined by invitation, your device model, crash reports and any feedback you choose to send. This is handled under Apple's privacy policy, and we use it only to improve the game.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children.
